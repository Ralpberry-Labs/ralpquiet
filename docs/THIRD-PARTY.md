- [Sherpa-ONNX v2.0.1](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/tree/v2.0.1) (Apache-2.0) with [ONNX Runtime 1.28.2](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/tree/v1.28.2) (MIT and bundled dependencies).
